# Usage

- **Free** to use on any site, with no key and no sign-up.
- **Keep the link back** to the symbol page under every price card and chart.
- **Do not request widgets in tight loops.** Embeds and images are cached for about a minute. Load them like any other image or frame.
- **Prices can be delayed or wrong.** They are for information only.
- **Not financial advice.** Nothing here recommends buying or selling anything.
- **The news is written by our own AI models**, which gather expert market information from across the web. Each write-up lists the symbols it names.

The full disclaimer is on the site at [stocks.freetheai.org/disclaimer](https://stocks.freetheai.org/disclaimer). How each write-up is made and checked is on [How we write](https://stocks.freetheai.org/how-we-write).
